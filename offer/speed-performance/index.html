The Problem

Your application loads in 6, 8, maybe 12 seconds. Users open it, stare at a loading spinner, and leave. Google penalises your SEO. Your infrastructure costs keep climbing because you keep throwing more servers at a fundamentally broken architecture.

Database queries run without indexes. APIs make 47 sequential calls instead of 3 parallel ones. Images load at full resolution on mobile. Every layer of your stack has performance debt that compounds into a terrible user experience.

You've tried caching. You've tried a CDN. Nothing moves the needle because the problems are architectural, not cosmetic.

Our Solution

We re-architect your backend, database, and API layer from the ground up for speed. Our performance engineers identify every bottleneck, optimise every query, and rebuild the critical paths to deliver sub-2-second load times under any traffic conditions.

We don't just make your app faster — we make it resilient. It handles 5,000 concurrent users without breaking a sweat, scales automatically during traffic spikes, and maintains performance as your user base grows.

How It Works

  • Week 1: Performance Audit — We profile your entire stack, identify every bottleneck, and create a prioritised optimisation roadmap.
  • Week 2-3: Backend Rebuild — We re-architect database queries, optimise API endpoints, and implement intelligent caching layers.
  • Week 4: Stress Testing — We run comprehensive load tests simulating real-world traffic to validate performance under pressure.
  • Week 4: Launch & Monitor — We deploy the optimised stack and monitor performance in real-time for the first 7 days.

The Guarantee

We guarantee your application will load in under 2 seconds and pass a stress test of 5,000 concurrent users. If it doesn't, the entire project is free. Pinnacle Consulting's app went from 8-second load times to 1.4 seconds — they haven't lost a customer since.
